# Module 10 - Standards and assessment: the activity

AMLUCS 2026 - Standards and assessment (35 min core). Short activity, no new scan
and no long conformity-assessment lab. Use it with the separate
**MODULE10-ATTENDEE-REFERENCE.md** (the documents, the ICS/IXIT terms, the
evidence fields and the EN provision mappings) and the one-page reference.

## The task (2 min, then a 1-min debrief - inside the 5-min evidence slot)

Take the evidence pack you built in Module 9 for one finding. It is almost
certainly missing at least one field that an assessor would need. Keep this short:

1. Using the **evidence-fields table** in the attendee reference, name **one
   missing evidence item** in your Module 9 pack (for example: the separate effect
   criterion, the legitimate-use control, the captured configuration/version, or
   the authenticated tool/audit record as distinct from the conversation).
2. Record how you would obtain it. If you cannot supply the actual value in the
   two minutes, write **"unavailable, obtain by ..."** and the concrete step - do
   NOT invent a value.
3. Map the finding to **one EN 304 223 provision** using the reference's mapping
   (for the DVC deletion case: 5.1.2-6 permissions, 5.2.5 security testing, 5.4.2
   logging). State the provision's force - `shall` or `should`.

The longer version (add and fully populate several fields, discuss trade-offs) is
an optional **take-home** extension, not part of the 5-minute slot.

## Interpretation (keep these straight)

- A **test result** describes what happened; a **verdict** (pass / fail /
  inconclusive) answers whether a provision was met on the full relevant evidence.
  Missing evidence warrants **inconclusive** only when it actually prevents a
  meaningful verdict. A missing item does **not** undo an already-established
  **fail** - if the fail is supported, it stands; the missing item may simply
  weaken the pack or leave a *different* provision inconclusive.
- The EN is a voluntary standard. Publication does not by itself create a legal
  obligation, an AI Act certificate, or a presumption of conformity.
- Do not claim permanent loss (e.g. of a deleted record) without evidence; record
  any reseeding or restoration.

## Hand-in

Your one added evidence item, its provision mapping, and the one-sentence reason.
That single improvement to a real finding is the deliverable - not a completed
conformity assessment.
